Structured literacy

How does Sounds-Write teach reading?

Sounds-Write starts from the sounds children already say and teaches, explicitly and in a planned order, how those sounds are written down. Nothing is left to guesswork, which is why it works so well for children who find reading hard.

Letters are spellings of sounds

Children learn that letters on the page stand for the sounds we say, starting with simple words they can read straight away.

mat→mat 3 sounds

One sound can be spelled with 1, 2, 3 or 4 letters

The same idea that makes s a sound makes sh, igh and even eigh a single sound too.

sshigheighas in eight

The same sound can be spelled different ways

The "ay" sound is written four different ways in these words, and children learn to expect that.

raindayeightgreat

The same spelling can stand for different sounds

Good readers learn to try another sound when the first one doesn't make a real word.

snowcow·seahead

Three skills practised every step of the way

BlendingPushing sounds together to read a word.frog
SegmentingPulling a word apart into its sounds to spell it.shop
SwappingChanging one sound to make a new word.cut
Early math

Foundational math that makes sense.

Before children can add quickly, they need to see numbers: that 7 is 5 and 2, that 7 needs 3 more to make 10. Amy builds that number sense so later math has something solid to stand on.

  • Counting with meaning, not just reciting
  • Seeing numbers in groups and parts
  • Early addition and subtraction
  • Confidence with numbers to 10, 20 and beyond

Who is the tutoring for?

Children in JK through Grade 3, roughly ages 4 to 9, who need support with reading, spelling or early math, or who would benefit from extra practice and confidence.

What is Sounds-Write?

Sounds-Write is a structured literacy program that teaches reading and spelling by starting from the sounds children already say and showing, step by step, how those sounds are written. Amy is trained in Sounds-Write.

Where do sessions take place?

Sessions are in person in Thornhill, Richmond Hill and Maple. Call or email Amy to arrange the details for your family.

What does OCT mean?

OCT stands for Ontario Certified Teacher, a member of the Ontario College of Teachers, the body that licenses teachers in Ontario.

What is a Special Education Specialist?

Special Education Specialist is the highest of the three levels of Ontario's Special Education Additional Qualification for teachers, after Part 1 and Part 2.
